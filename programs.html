Our programs

Programs that build support, education, and opportunity.

Our programs combine mentorship, service, and advocacy to create lasting support for youth in foster care and the communities around them.

Get involved See our work
What we offer

Our programs are grounded in support, service, and meaningful connection.

We create opportunities for students and youth to learn, grow, and show up for one another. There are many ways to get involved and make a difference, here are a few.

01

Fellowship Program

Committed members work closely with the executive board on projects that advance our mission and expand impact.

02

IRC Mentorship

Our mentors support youth in college and career exploration, goal setting, and post-secondary preparation.

03

Community Events

We participate in off-campus events, campus service projects, and partnerships that build awareness and connection.

04

Advocacy Initiatives

We engage in policy advocacy and community organizing to support systemic change for youth in foster care.

05

Speaker Events

Hear from professionals, advocates, and individuals with lived experience as they share insights on foster care, child welfare, and advocacy.

Join us

There is a place for every student who wants to help.

No previous experience is required. We provide training, resources, and community support for new members.
