Our impact

Our impact grows through consistent service, trusted relationships, and shared action.

We focus on measurable support for youth in foster care by mobilizing student volunteers, building campus awareness, and connecting community partners to needs that matter right now.

Student-led action

Campus volunteers turn intention into real support.

Our members organize drives, service projects, and outreach efforts that directly benefit youth and families connected to the foster care system.

Community outcomes

We strengthen networks that last beyond one event.

By partnering with local organizations and advocates, we help extend resources, increase awareness, and create sustainable pathways for ongoing support.

Upcoming and recent events

Each event helps us grow our support network for youth in foster care.

From gift drives to campus partnerships, our events create moments of care and community.

Annual Gift Drive

Seasonal drives that provide gifts and care packages for youth in foster care.

  • 70 gifts raised in 2024
  • 100 gifts raised in 2025

Comfort Cases

We partner with Comfort Cases, founded by Rob Scheer, to give youth in foster care backpacks filled with essential supplies. No child deserves to carry their life in a trash bag and we ensure they don't have to.

  • 30 backpacks packed in 2024
  • 60 backpacks packed in 2025

Campus and Community Outreach

Volunteer days and partnerships that widen awareness while strengthening local support.

  • FAPAC Summit: a free, one-day event in partnership with FAPAC, where parents and youth ages 16-21 in DC foster care. From mentoring, games, tours and informational sessions; our goal was to inspire teens for life after the foster system.
  • Hands-on volunteer events on and off campus, speaker events and mentorship opportunities

Fundraising and Awareness

Events like flower sales and donor campaigns create the resources needed to sustain our work.

  • $881 raised in 2025 flower sale
  • Support for education-focused initiatives
