Community, care, and support

Empowering youth in foster care through mentorship, advocacy, and campus service.

Terps Fostering Futures (formerly Legal Guardian Angels) connects UMD students with youth in foster care who need guidance, support, and access to trusted resources.

About us

What we do

We’re University of Maryland students working toward one goal: creating stability for youth in foster care. Children and teenagers entering, moving through, or exiting care face a level of adversity most will never have to consider.

Through hands-on service, philanthropy, and advocacy, we address their unique set of needs while investing in their long-term success. We know that consistent support systems, whether in areas like education, housing, or healthcare, is what changes outcomes.

How it comes together

Support that feels personal, practical, and consistent.

We pair mission-driven student leadership with real community action so youth in foster care are met with encouragement, resources, and long-term care.

Programs

TFF believes strong education and stable support systems create better futures for youth in foster care.

We work to increase access to resources, improve legal understanding, and build a caring campus community.

01

Membership

Each member receives education and support to effectively engage with youth in foster care and contribute to our mission. GBMs are also a great way to meet like-minded peers, build meaningful connections, and form lasting friendships.

Volunteer opportunities

Get involved by volunteering at events, supporting donation drives, assisting with fundraising and outreach, and partnering with organizations serving youth in foster care. Every contribution helps us support youth in foster and strengthen our community.
