Our partners

We work alongside organizations that strengthen support for youth in foster care.

These partnerships help us expand resources, bring in trusted expertise, and build a stronger network of care.

Community collaboration

FAPAC DC

We have collaborated with FAPAC DC on various initiatives to support youth in foster care, including community events and volunteer opportunities. FAPAC is based in DC whose mission is to advocate for families and youth in foster care and give them a voice.

Advocacy and outreach

Children in Need of Assistance (CINA)

CINA is a child welfare intervention program to protect severely vulnerable children. Our work with fellow advocates helps expand awareness and educate those involved about the needs and rights of youth in foster care.

Terps Fostering Futures

Student-led nonprofit supporting youth in foster care through volunteer work and advocacy.
