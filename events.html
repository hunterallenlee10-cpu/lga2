Our events

Community events that connect service, generosity, and care.

We host gatherings throughout the year that bring students, mentors, and supporters together around youth in foster care.

Support the mission See photos
Upcoming and past events

Each event helps us grow our support network for youth in foster care.

From gift drives to campus partnerships, our events create moments of care and community.

Speaker Events

Panels and talks featuring advocates, professionals, and people with lived experience in foster care.

  • Some of our speakers have included: Maryland Supreme Court Justice Angela Eaves, Comfort Cases founder Rob Scheer, and CINA attorney Richard Perry.

Hands-on Volunteer Events

Service projects where members and supporters assemble resources and provide practical support.

  • Christmas for Kids with FAPAC: Provided support for a reunification event bringing together approximately 300 siblings in foster care.
  • On-site volunteer days with partner organizations.

Fundraising Initiatives

Community campaigns and student-led fundraisers that sustain our programs year-round.

  • 2025 Flower Sale: $881 raised, funding 3 Dell laptops for youth pursuing their education.
  • Over $2,000 raised through various campus campaigns in 2024-2025.

Mentorship Programs

Relationship-centered events that connect youth with mentors for academic, career, and life guidance.

  • International Rescue Committee Mentors: this program pairs dedicated volunteers with recently resettled youth and young adults for one-on-one mentorship focused on college and career preparation.
  • And more to come!

Fellowship Programs

Leadership-focused opportunities where committed members co-lead initiatives and deepen service impact.

  • Our new Fellowship Program: Fellows are a pool of committed members dedicated to strengethening our mission, at the end of the semester, they have the opportunity to apply to work directly under an executive member.
  • Training in advocacy, outreach, and community leadership
